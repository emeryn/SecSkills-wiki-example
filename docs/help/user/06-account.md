# Your account

Open the user menu (top right) > **Account**.

- **Password**: at least 12 characters. Long passphrases work best; common or leaked passwords are
  refused. Changing it signs you out everywhere else.
- **Two-factor authentication**: add an authenticator app or a security key (passkey). Keep your
  recovery codes somewhere safe: each one works once.
- **Sessions**: see where you are signed in, and sign out other devices.

Administrators must use two-factor authentication.
