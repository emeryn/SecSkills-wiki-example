# Spaces and pages

- Open a space to see its page tree in the sidebar; the space home page opens first.
- **New page** creates a page at the top level, or under the page you are reading.
- **Move**: choose a new parent. A page tree is at most 5 levels deep.
- **Delete** moves the page and its sub-pages to the space **trash**. Editors can restore them for
  30 days; after that they are deleted for good.
- **Export** downloads a page, or the whole space, as Markdown files in a zip.

## Space settings (space admins)

Rename the space, change its visibility (*restricted*: members only; *internal*: every signed-in
user can read) and manage its members. Members can be users or groups.
