# Getting started

The wiki is organized in **spaces**. Each space holds a tree of **pages** written in Markdown.

- **Dashboard** (`/`): your favorite pages, recent changes and the spaces you belong to.
- **Spaces**: every space you can read. Restricted spaces are visible to their members only.
- **Search** (`Ctrl+K` or `/`): full-text search across every page you can read.
- **Favorites**: star a page to pin it in the sidebar.
- **Help**: the `(?)` button in the top bar opens the help for the page you are on.

## Roles in a space

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Viewer | Read pages and attachments |
| Editor | Also create, edit, move and delete pages, upload attachments |
| Space admin | Also manage the members and settings of the space |

Ask a space admin for access to a restricted space.
